# VOXTASK screen brief

Design a responsive desktop-first web app for voice-to-task capture. Dark near-black background (#222222), off-white Inter-style type, restrained hairline borders, bright red as the active voice color. Take visual inspiration from the circular animation at animejs.com, including its six glowing outer segments (red, orange, green, cyan, blue, lime), fine red radial ticks, dark concentric tracks, warm inner arc, red horizontal waveform lines, and a red dotted motion path. The circle is the primary RECORD button, approximately 400px on desktop and 280px on mobile. Its outer colored segments rotate slowly after load; center line waves move smoothly and continuously.

First load: black screen, circle assembles alone from a partial red tick arc into all rings. Only after the circle finishes does the rest of the UI fade in. Empty history state after entrance: a narrow top navbar and the centered record circle dominate the first viewport. The navbar contains VOXTASK logo, Tasks, How it works, and a small Settings control. A short caption under the circle says "Tap to capture a thought"; no large marketing hero, cards, forms, or task list in the first viewport.

When a user has saved tasks or scrolls to their tasks, the same circle smoothly shrinks and docks in a fixed bottom-center control so the list has room. Show a clear, content-first task history section below the hero with grouped task cards, due date/constraint, priority, and status. Recording state should make the center red waveform respond more strongly and expose a Stop control; after recording, show editable transcript and extracted tasks. Keep advanced test controls such as microphone selection, vocabulary hints, raw JSON, and calibration reviews in a collapsed Settings/Diagnostics area, not in the opening view.

Maintain strong text contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, a reduced-motion fallback, and touch targets of at least 44px. Use deliberate spacing and subtle motion. This is a usable productivity app, not a landing page.
